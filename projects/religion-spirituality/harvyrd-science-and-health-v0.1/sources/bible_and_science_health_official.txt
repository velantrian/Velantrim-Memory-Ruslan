The Bible and Science and Health
Christian Science is accessible to all—there are no mysterious teachings
Everything to know about Christian Science can be found in the Bible and
Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures
by Mary Baker Eddy. There are no secret teachings or doctrines in Christian Science. All of the resources provided by the Church are avenues to share how the teachings of the Bible and
Science and Health
are being lived, and how the example of Jesus can be followed today
The Bible's central message is opened up by
Science and Health
The Bible is foundational to Christian Science. Mary Baker Eddy, the Discoverer and Founder of Christian Science, actively studied the Bible her whole life. Her book
Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures
opens up the spiritual message of the Bible. Often referred to as the “textbook” of Christian Science,
Science and Health
supports one’s practical application of the Bible’s message, without replacing the Bible itself.
Core to the theology, Church, and individual practice
The Bible and
Science and Health
are the primary texts of Christian Science.
Christian Science theology is found in the ideas presented in these books, especially in Jesus’ teachings.
All church services include readings from these books.
Individuals read and study these books to learn about God and their relationship to God. This study often includes the weekly Bible Lessons, which are made up solely of citations from these books.
People have been healed by reading these books
People across various faiths love the Bible. One reason the Bible and
Science and Health
are so important to Christian Scientists is because many people have been healed and transformed through the ideas and insights they’ve gained by reading them.
