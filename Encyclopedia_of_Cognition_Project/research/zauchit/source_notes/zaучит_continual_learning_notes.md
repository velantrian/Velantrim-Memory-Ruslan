Источники и проверенные факты
1. Dohare et al., Nature 2024, Loss of plasticity in deep continual learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07711-7. ImageNet/CIFAR-100, permuted MNIST, RL; стандартные методы теряют способность учиться при длительном потоке. Continual backprop reinitializes малую долю low-utility units; поддерживает plasticity. Отлично от CF: LoP — не может учить новое, CF — падает старое. Ограничение: benchmarks часто почти не повторяют старые задачи, forgetting/transfer не измеряются; метод не решает forgetting.
2. Elsayed et al., ICLR 2024, Addressing loss of plasticity and catastrophic forgetting in continual learning. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/8e5f0591943d8dae5702af12dcdcd2f6-Abstract-Conference.html. UPGD: utility-based perturbations; protects useful units, perturbs less useful. Streaming hundreds nonstationarities, unknown boundaries; surpasses/competitive; PPO avoids Adam drop. Abstract lacks detailed benchmark limits.
3. Wang et al., NeurIPS 2021, Active Forgetting of Negative Transfer in Continual Learning. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/bc6dc48b743dc5d013b1abaebd2faed2-Abstract.html. AFEC actively forgets old knowledge that interferes with new; synaptic expansion-convergence/Bayesian CL; CIFAR-10 regression, visual classification, Atari; improves new-task learning/SOTA. Limits: method-specific benchmarks, not proof of biological mechanism.
4. Holton et al./chapter, Continual Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting, arXiv 2403.05175 (2024). https://arxiv.org/html/2403.05175v1. Defines CL on nonstationary stream; CF as rapid drastic old-info loss, likely parameter shift; stability-plasticity tradeoff; stopping CF insufficient; intentional forgetting may be useful under bounded resources. Review/chapter, not new meta-analysis.
5. Zhang et al., Machine unlearning: A comprehensive survey, arXiv 2405.07406 (2024). https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.07406. Defines removal of erased subset contribution; exact vs approximate; centralized/distributed/irregular, verification, privacy/security. Limits/open problems: guarantees and verification, not a continual-learning behavioral study.
