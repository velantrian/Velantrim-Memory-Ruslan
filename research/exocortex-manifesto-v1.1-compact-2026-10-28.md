## ⚖️ Eleven principles

| # | Principle | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 01 👤 | Authorship | Model proposals never silently become user decisions |
| 02 🔍 | Evidence | Source integrity is not truth |
| 03 🚨 | Five drift classes | No silent promotion of authorship, evidence, status, applicability, or consensus |
| 04 ⚖️ | Critique | Criticism itself needs independently checkable grounds |
| 05 🧠 | Three memory layers | Episode, revisable lesson, applicability boundary |
| 06 🔗 | Four relations | Authorship, provenance, warrant, authority separately |
| 07 📚 | Two histories | Human decisions and agent operations remain distinct |
| 08 🔄 | End-to-end cycle | Research target: test whether experience affects later behavior after restart; NOT ESTABLISHED HERE |
| 09 🛡️ | Freedom and boundaries | Explore hypotheses; act only within granted authority |
| 10 🧪 | Verification | Model agreement is not independent evidence |
| 11 🌱 | Utility | Reduce human burden without reducing quality or safety |

## 🚨 Five drift classes

Authorship: model suggestion becomes alleged owner decision.
Evidence: reported tool call becomes alleged verified result.
Status: hypothesis becomes fact.
Applicability: local lesson becomes universal rule.
Cascade: repeated reports of one source become alleged independent confirmation.

## 🔬 Research boundary

This document does not establish an implemented exocortex, validated memory transfer, subjective experience, experimental success, new architecture, canonical policy, or authorization to run experiments. A corrected chat reply does not prove persistence after restart. A successful end-to-end demonstration is necessary but insufficient without reliability, safety and measurable user benefit. Historical episodes are subject to correction, deletion and revoked permissions. Five *drift classes* are not the same as five recorded *goal-drift episodes*.

**Research question:** When does past experience help or harm a new decision, and can justified corrections persist after restart?

Proposed comparison (NOT RUN, NOT PREREGISTERED): A no history; B plaintext history; C structured memory; D superficially similar case with changed conditions; E outdated conclusion plus correction.
