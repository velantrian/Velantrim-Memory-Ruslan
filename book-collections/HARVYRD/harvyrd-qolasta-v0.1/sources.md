# Sources and editorial boundaries

These are the seven cited sources used by the Russian reader guide. The numbered markers in the book correspond to this list.

- [1] Kurt Rudolph, “Mandaeans II. The Mandaean Religion,” Encyclopaedia Iranica. [https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/mandaeans/mandaeans-ii-the-mandaean-religion/](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/mandaeans/mandaeans-ii-the-mandaean-religion/) — Reference overview for religion, corpus and liturgical functions.
- [2] Mark Lidzbarski, Mandäische Liturgien, HathiTrust catalogue record. [https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/103093940](https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/103093940) — Bibliographic metadata and volume contents; not a critical apparatus.
- [3] E. S. Drower, The Canonical Prayerbook of the Mandaeans (1959), Archive.org OCR. [https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.530195/2015.530195.canonical-prayer_djvu.txt](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.530195/2015.530195.canonical-prayer_djvu.txt) — Published edition/translation and editorial introduction; OCR may contain recognition errors.
- [4] M. Arabestani, “Ritual purity and the Mandaeans’ identity,” Brill (abstract). [https://brill.com/view/journals/ic/16/2/article-p153_2.xml](https://brill.com/view/journals/ic/16/2/article-p153_2.xml) — Abstract only; used for a broad social-context point, not detailed claims about prayers.
- [5] Jorunn Jacobsen Buckley, “The Colophons in the Canonical Prayerbook of the Mandaeans,” JSTOR. [https://www.jstor.org/stable/545596](https://www.jstor.org/stable/545596) — Study concerning colophons in the prayerbook; access may be restricted.
- [6] Jorunn Jacobsen Buckley, “The Colophons in H. Petermann’s Sidra Rabba,” Cambridge. [https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-royal-asian-society/article/colophons-in-h-petermanns-sidrarabba/16B8984FF6EC3AF1C35466B256C380F2](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-royal-asian-society/article/colophons-in-h-petermanns-sidrarabba/16B8984FF6EC3AF1C35466B256C380F2) — Used only as general colophon/transmission context, not as direct evidence for a particular Qolasta copy.
- [7] University of Exeter Mandaean Priests Project, “Academic Studies of the Mandaeans”. [https://mandaeanpriests.exeter.ac.uk/academic-studies-of-the-mandaeans](https://mandaeanpriests.exeter.ac.uk/academic-studies-of-the-mandaeans) — Overview of academic access and collaboration with living communities; not a survey of every contemporary community.

## Editorial boundaries

The guide is a reader-oriented account of the Qolasta as described by the cited reference and editions. It is not a translation, critical edition, prayer book, or ritual manual. It does not reproduce prayer formulas or reconstruct the sequence of masbuta or masiqta. The text distinguishes manuscript witness, editorial choice, community practice, and scholarly interpretation.

The source set does not establish a complete stemma or a single original order for every manuscript, the full history of every prayer, or the practices of every present-day Mandaean community. Drower’s OCR may contain recognition errors; the HathiTrust record is bibliographic rather than a critical apparatus; the Brill source is an abstract; and some scholarly sources may be access-limited. Claims concerning ritual access and priestly knowledge are attributed to their specific cited context, not generalized into a universal rule.

The five original illustrations are visual metaphors, not manuscript photographs or reconstructions of religious rites.
